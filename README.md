## Hi, I'm Marissa!

Thanks for stopping by! I'm a developer on the web team at Saddleback Church. I work on [saddleback.com](https://saddleback.com/) with a small team of front-end and back-end devs, a PM, and our designer.

I started at Saddleback as an admin assistant on the Communications team. Working with our web designers and developers is what got me curious about coding! I started learning on Team Treehouse and Udemy, then did the LearningFuze bootcamp part time and finished in 2022. I moved over to the web team in 2023. Most of what I do is front-end work in Vue. I really enjoy taking our designer's Figma designs and making sure everything looks right on the site. I usually partner with a back-end dev on bigger features, and I'm excited to get more hands-on with the back end and APIs too.

### What I work with

- Vue.js, JavaScript, HTML, CSS/SCSS, Razor
- Umbraco CMS and Rock RMS
- GitHub, Azure, Postman, VS Code, Figma

### Some things I've worked on

My work repos are private, but here's some of it live:

- We rebuilt saddleback.com from scratch and moved it to a new Umbraco CMS. There were three of us on it. We launched the main pages first, then moved everything else over until the old site was gone.
- [Watch Live](https://saddleback.com/watch/live/). We switched video platforms, and I built the front end for live and on-demand video using APIs from our back-end devs.

I'm also on our release rotation, so some weeks I'm the one pushing builds from dev to stage to prod.
